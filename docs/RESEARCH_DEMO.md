## What happens once a provider key is added

1. *Find decision-makers* asks each enabled provider for people at the developer whose titles match the priority list.
2. Apollo returns first name, partly hidden surname and title at no credit cost; **Reveal** spends about 1 credit for the full name, email and LinkedIn URL. Hunter returns people found on the company's public web pages, citing each page.
3. Every candidate is scored out of 100 (responsibility 35, project connection 30, authority 20, verified contact details 15) with a reason and source for each part. Project connection is capped below full marks until someone confirms the person actually works on this project.
4. Candidates stay **Unverified leads** until the email passes verification or you paste a LinkedIn profile you checked. Then *Save to CRM* creates the contact, deduplicated by email and name+company, linked to the project and company.
5. *Draft email* picks the style from the role (estimator → bid response; property manager → maintenance; developer/GC → short and direct) and uses only verified facts. Nothing is sent without approval.
